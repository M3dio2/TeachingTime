# TeachingTime: demo setup for screenshots and video

## 1. Accounts and devices
- Teacher: a separate account named like a real teacher, e.g. "Ms. Rivera" (not your own, so nothing private is visible).
- Laptop for the teacher: browser window at 1440 × 900 (or full-screen), zoom 100 %, bookmarks bar hidden, no other tabs.
- Phone as the student: battery and notifications hidden (Do Not Disturb), brightness high.
- Light: window or lamp in front of you, plain background, both cameras on (people sell the app, empty tiles don't).

## 2. Dashboard (screenshot 1)
Create 3 Classrooms, so it looks lived-in but not cluttered:
- **Lucía · English B1**
- **Tom · German A2**
- **Amélie · Spanish beginners**
Put the worksheet PDF and one picture into Lucía's Classroom.

## 3. Quiz (screenshot 2: the editor)
Title: **Past simple: irregular verbs**
1. Multiple choice: "Yesterday I ___ to the cinema." → went ✔ / goed / have gone
2. True or false: "'Bought' is the past of 'bring'." → False (explanation: "It's 'brought'. 'Bought' comes from 'buy'.")
3. Choose in the text (gaps with options): "Last summer we [flew | flied] to Lisbon and [stayed | stood] near the sea."
4. Match the pairs: eat–ate, drink–drank, write–wrote, swim–swam
5. Put in order: I · saw · an old friend · yesterday
Keep question 3 open in the editor when you take the screenshot (shows the editing options).

## 4. Lesson (screenshots 3–6)
Start the lesson with Lucía's Classroom, join on the phone.
- **3 · Two tabs:** worksheet PDF left (write "went" into the first gap with the pen, in red), whiteboard right with a quick drawing: a little timeline "yesterday ← now" with an arrow.
- **4 · Quiz live:** drag the quiz onto the stage, answer 3 questions on the phone (one wrong!), so the teacher sees right/wrong.
- **5 · Phone view:** screenshot on the phone while the quiz is open (the student side).
- **6 · Just talk:** both cameras big, smiling. Good as the "hero" picture.
Optional: a YouTube clip in a tab (e.g. a short song for the lesson topic).

## 5. Before every screenshot
- Close the files panel unless it's part of the shot.
- No mouse pointer in the middle of the picture.
- Language: English for Reddit / Product Hunt; a German version for the DaF groups is a nice extra.
