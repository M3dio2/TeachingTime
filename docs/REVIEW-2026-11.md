# Full review, 25 Nov 2026

Method: every screen at 1440 px and 390 px (`/opt/data/cache/scratch/l1shots/audit.spec.ts`),
axe-core accessibility scan on each, console errors, a code/route inventory, and the
ui-ux-pro-max checks (accessibility, touch targets, hierarchy, feedback, destructive actions,
loading, onboarding).

Result in short: solid and accessible (axe found no critical issues, no console errors), but
the app is missing several processes a paying teacher expects, and a few screens have weak
hierarchy.

## A. Missing processes (functional)

| # | What | Why | Size |
|---|------|-----|------|
| A1 | Try it without signing up (demo room with a fake student) | Biggest conversion lever; Product Hunt / Show HN expect it | M |
| A2 | Noticing a waiting student: sound + "(1) Anna is waiting" in the tab title + optional browser notification | Today only a badge, checked every 10 s; teachers miss students | S |
| A3 | Test lesson with yourself ("Open the student link on your phone") + device check for the teacher before the lesson | First lesson is where trust is won or lost; the teacher has no camera/mic check | S |
| A4 | Next lesson per student (date/time), shown on the card; .ics for the teacher, a reminder email with the link to the student (optional address) | Weekly rhythm is the core use case; no scheduling at all | M |
| A5 | Lesson notes / history per Classroom: dates and length (data exists in lesson_sessions), keep the text pad as a dated note, export as PDF/Word for the student | Text and board are wiped after 3 h; students take nothing home | M |
| A6 | Account self-service: change email and password, delete account (with all files), download my data | GDPR promise in the privacy policy is "write to us" today | S–M |
| A7 | Onboarding checklist on the dashboard (add Classroom → send link → test lesson → upload a file), hides when done | Empty dashboard explains little | S |
| A8 | Error alerts (Sentry free) + weekly database backup | Errors land in a table nobody reads; Supabase free has no point-in-time restore | S |
| A9 | Pricing page + payment later (Stripe), plan limits shown in Settings | Needed before LiveKit/Vercel costs grow; Vercel Hobby is non-commercial | M |
| A10 | Lesson chat (send a link or a word without opening a tab) | Most-used feature in Zoom lessons; the text pad is overkill for one word | S |
| A11 | Connection quality hint ("Your connection is weak") | Students on mobile data; today only "Reconnecting" | S |
| A12 | Storage: 50 MB total is tight now that videos are allowed | One phone video fills a third of it | decision |

## B. UX/UI findings

Dashboard
- B1 Two competing primary actions: "Start a quick call" is the only filled button, but the main job is "Start lesson" on a Classroom card. Make Start lesson the filled one; Quick call secondary.
- B2 Header has 5 items (language, settings, feedback, name, log out); on a phone 3 are icon-only. One avatar menu: Settings, Language, Feedback, Log out.
- B3 The student card shows "No files yet" but not when the next lesson is (see A4) or whether the student is online now (only during a call).

Lesson room (teacher)
- B4 Own camera is as big as the student's and the left column wastes width; make the student big and self-view small.
- B5 "Anna views only" is a state written like an action. Better a switch "Anna can write" (on/off), like on the phone.
- B6 The empty text pad has no hint. Placeholder: "Type here. Anna sees it as you type."
- B7 The student link isn't reachable inside the lesson once the student left (re-invite).
- B8 The bottom bar left label "Lesson with …" is easy to miss; the lesson timer only appears near the 3 h limit. A small elapsed time helps teachers bill/plan.

Lesson room (student, phone)
- B9 Mic and camera buttons are icon-only, no labels/state text ("Mic off").
- B10 Large empty white area when the teacher shows an empty text pad; a short "Your teacher will show something here" helps.

Landing page
- B11 The hero shows a drawing, not the real product; a 20 s loop of a real lesson converts better.
- B12 No social proof yet (one quote from a tester, number of lessons held).
- B13 The secondary "Log in" button in the hero competes with the CTA; logged-out teachers have it in the header already.

Feedback & safety
- B14 Deletes use the browser's own confirm box. In-app dialog naming the item ("Delete worksheet.pdf"), and Undo for file deletes.
- B15 Long operations (upload, cloud fetch) show only text; a progress bar for uploads over 2 MB.

Accessibility (axe-core, all minor or moderate)
- B16 Logo link and language button: accessible name differs from visible text.
- B17 Lesson page has no main landmark and no h1 (screen readers).
- B18 Small tap targets on phones (< 44 px): "Forgot your password?", "Create a teacher account", footer links, terms/privacy links in sign-up.

## C. Suggested order

1. Quick wins, one branch (about 1 day): A2, A3, A7, B1, B2, B5, B6, B9, B14, B16–B18.
2. Teacher retention: A4 next lesson + reminders, A5 notes/history.
3. Growth: A1 demo room, B11 real hero video, B12 social proof.
4. Trust & business: A6 account self-service, A8 alerts/backups, A9 pricing.
