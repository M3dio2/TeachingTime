Presentations: PDF + a “Slides” view. PowerPoint, Keynote and Google Slides all export PDF in two clicks (the upload now explains how). In the lesson a PDF gets a “Slides” switch: one slide fills the stage, ← → or the buttons move both screens, and the pen still writes on it. Native .pptx (animations, fonts) would need a paid converter and still look off, so I’d leave it out.
